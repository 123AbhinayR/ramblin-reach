# Sponsor-aligned pitch, defensibility, and 3-minute demo

## North star

**Ramblin' Reach — Learn from the campus. Learn from each other. Pass it forward.**

**The specific problem:** Georgia Tech students may know that makerspaces, career programs, clubs and alumni networks exist, yet struggle to combine them into a practical next step for *their* goal. People graduate and take useful know-how with them unless they voluntarily document it. Ramblin' Reach uses a small reviewed resource catalog and a consent-based member network to bridge both gaps.

**Do not pitch** `AI alumni LinkedIn`, `the first mentoring search`, `we scraped 200,000 alumni`, `first ever request router`, or `guaranteed jobs/referrals`. Existing GT Connect/Mentor Jackets, PeopleGrove, Stanford and Harvard address parts of alumni discovery and mentoring. Our distinction is the **connected learner journey**: `goal → ordered, source-backed resources → relevant opted-in human help → structured request → optional reusable lesson`.

## How this fits the College of Lifetime Learning

Strategy 2035 emphasizes learning across the lifespan, learner-first systems, and communities linking people, industry and research. Ramblin' Reach creates a small observable lifelong loop:
1. **Learn by doing (JacketPath):** Understand how to act on a goal, including facility eligibility and prerequisites.
2. **Learn from people (JacketMatch):** Connect to student builders and alumni who opted in, including transitions to a new employer or city.
3. **Pass learning forward (ReachCard + opt-in lesson):** Ask well, complete a conversation, and preserve one concise experience as a portfolio/lesson that stays with its contributor as they graduate.

This is **not** a new LMS or degree platform; it is a navigation and peer-knowledge layer. It can complement existing University services only with approved links/integrations.

## 55–65 second pitch

> Georgia Tech has incredible resources: makerspaces, project teams, career services, and alumni with years of experience. But when a student wants to build something or move into a new opportunity, knowing those resources exist isn't the same as knowing how to put them together.
>
> Ramblin' Reach turns one goal into a practical pathway. JacketPath recommends verified campus resources and ordered next steps. JacketMatch finds students and alumni who have relevant experience and are actually accepting requests, including filters by city and company. ReachCard helps students ask one thoughtful question and makes it easy for a volunteer to respond.
>
> The long-term vision is lifelong learning: what one Jacket learns today can become a small project lesson and a connection that helps the next generation, even after graduation. We're not replacing GT Connect or campus makerspaces; we're connecting existing strengths into one actionable journey.
>
> Ramblin' Reach: Learn from the campus. Learn from each other. Pass it forward.

## The competitive landscape (speak carefully)

| Alternative | Real role | Our focus |
|---|---|---|
| Georgia Tech Connect / Mentor Jackets | Networking and mentoring | Start with a concrete goal and bridge resources + consented expertise + next action |
| GT Hive / Invention Studio | Equipment, training and prototype help | Guide the *path* to the relevant official resource, not replace safety/access rules |
| Harvard / Stanford alumni tools | Strong alumni discovery and mentoring | A source-backed, project-led student workflow with opt-in micro-lessons and explicit city+company filter |
| Typical campus chatbot | Answers questions from documents | Deterministically orders verified steps, sends real requests through authorized accounts, and tracks user-reported progress |
| Social network or project wiki | Profiles / documents | Only enough project memory to match expertise and avoid repeating lessons, without a second product |

**Originality claim:** a particular *integration and workflow* is distinctive for the use case. Do not claim universal uniqueness or exclusive patents; we have not audited every product or evaluated GT Connect's full authenticated feature set.

## Judge questions and concise answers

**Q: Doesn't Georgia Tech already have GT Connect?** Yes, and it's valuable. We're not importing its private profiles. Ramblin' Reach combines relevant campus resource steps, opt-in expertise, a specific request, and an optional reusable lesson around a student's goal; an authorized future partnership could complement existing programs.

**Q: How do you get alumni without scraping?** Start with five consenting helpers (some student makers, some alumni) and a small pilot. Demo accounts are fictional and labeled. Expand only through voluntary opt-in or approved university integrations.

**Q: What makes the AI trustworthy?** The model extracts limited goal tags or offers optional writing feedback; only reviewed records become resource recommendations. Deterministic routing and matching work without AI. Every output has reasons and explicit limitations.

**Q: What if no alum at Microsoft in Seattle?** We show *no exact available match* and separately labeled alternatives such as Jackets in Seattle with company-agnostic career insight or Microsoft alumni in another city. We never mislabel people.

**Q: How do you avoid exhausting volunteers?** They select help types, control visibility, set monthly caps and pause participation; sending is transactionally limited at the database level, not just hidden behind a UI button.

**Q: Isn't your information stale?** Every source has an official link and review date; uncertain or out-of-date access conditions are labeled unknown. We do not claim live room hours or machine availability.

**Q: What happens when students graduate?** In future approved verification, their account/portfolio stays intact and affiliation changes after authorized review; they may decide to remain a volunteer. No automatic degree verification via guessed year.

**Q: Why this sponsor?** This is learning over a lifetime: hands-on campus exploration, peer and alumni knowledge exchange, then experience preserved across student cohorts. It aligns with lifelong access and communities, without promising institution-wide deployment.

**Q: How do you evaluate impact?** Track relevant match feedback, path completion (self-reported), response/acceptance and completed conversation rate, helper burden, and lesson reuse. Don't overclaim job outcomes from a request.

## Pilot plan and honest metrics

Pilot with 5–10 students, 3–5 opt-in helpers, 5–8 resource records chosen with campus operators. Obtain qualitative feedback on correctness; avoid users automatically sharing all portfolios. Report denominators and difference between demo, self-report and verified outcomes. Expand campus resources only after review agreements.
